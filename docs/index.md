# Tab-to-Task

**Save a webpage, remember why it matters, and return to it when you're ready to act.**

Your browser remembers the page. It forgets why you opened it. Tab-to-Task saves a page together
with its **next action**, so the tab doesn't have to stay open as a reminder.

- Press `Ctrl+Shift+S` to save the page you're on, with the one thing you meant to do about it.
- Press `Ctrl+Shift+E` to open your list in Chrome's side panel.
- Close the tab. The reminder lives here now.

## Privacy

No account, no server, no analytics, no network requests. Everything stays in your browser.

Read the full [Privacy Policy](privacy).
